5. In-app purchases

The app offers seventeen optional question packs, sold as non-consumable items: bought once, kept for good. The base game — six subjects, 2,400 questions — is complete without any of them.

  • What you are buying. The question files ship inside the app on every device. What a purchase unlocks is the right to select that pack in your own games. This is why a player joining a multi-device table can play the host's packs without having bought them: it is a deliberate design choice, and it is stated on the packs screen inside the app.
